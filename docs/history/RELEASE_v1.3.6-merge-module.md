# v1.3.6-merge-module — motion and copy fixes

XML Views only, single module (branch [`merge-module`](https://github.com/namvunhatle/r15-onboarding-android-demo/tree/merge-module)). One track, Ogg Opus, as in 1.3.5.

- **Splash progress** opens at 80 %, then eases to 100 % over the 5 s splash.
- **G01:** the rising phone stays on the bottom edge; its overshoot no longer leaves a gap.
- **G03:** the phone no longer jumps smaller when the screen switches to the call.
- **Bubble:** “Hey Sam calling…”, without the comma.

**Download:** `A7-XMLViews-1.3.6.apk` · Android 9+ · `namvunhatle.r15.onboarding.views.responsive` · installs over 1.3.5.

**Tested** on an Android 16 emulator at 20:9 and 19.5:9, and installed on a Pixel 5. Same fixes on `main`: [v1.3.6](https://github.com/namvunhatle/r15-onboarding-android-demo/releases/tag/v1.3.6).
